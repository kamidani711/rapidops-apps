Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Is extracting leads from Google Maps legal?

Yes. Commercial listings on Google Maps are publicly published business records designed specifically for public discovery. Scraping publicly available commercial directory data is protected under established US judicial rulings (such as hiQ Labs v. LinkedIn) and complies with commercial GDPR/CCPA regulations, provided you respect local outreach laws (CAN-SPAM, opt-out requests).

How does LeadRadar prevent Google IP bans and CAPTCHAs?

LeadRadar combines two protective layers: First, built-in Anti-Detect Human Pacing mimics realistic browser interactions with randomized delays. Second, for agencies extracting 1,000–5,000+ leads daily, LeadRadar includes a Built-In Proxy Tunnel with a live latency pinger. You can plug in any standard rotating residential proxy (Webshare, Bright Data, IPRoyal) to cycle exit IPs seamlessly.

How do the 1-Click Cold Pitch Hooks work?

LeadRadar analyzes the listing's public profile (website presence, star rating, total review volume, and niche category). It instantly crafts tailored outreach angles: if they lack a website, it generates web design pitches; if their reviews are below 4.0, it generates reputation management scripts; if they are top-rated, it generates local scaling offers.

What are the system requirements?

LeadRadar runs natively on Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit). It utilizes an isolated Chromium backend that operates with minimal system memory (~270MB RAM) without slowing down your everyday computer tasks.

How does the hardware licensing work?

When you purchase LeadRadar, you receive a cryptographic license key. The first time you activate the desktop app, it securely ties to your computer's unique hardware identifier (HWID). If you need to transfer your license to a new PC, our support team can reset your hardware binding instantly upon request.
