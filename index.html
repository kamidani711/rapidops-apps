Docket

100% On-Device · Zero Cloud · Android 7.0+

Docket is a private, lightning-fast document scanner built for Android. It uses Google’s on-device ML Kit neural models to auto-detect edges, flatten receipts, run instant OCR, place handwritten signatures, apply custom watermarks, and generate searchable PDFs without a single byte ever touching a remote server.

✓
ML Kit Auto-Scanner: Perspective deskew & real-time shadow removal.
✓
Live Text OCR: Interactive bounding boxes on every recognized word.
✓
Signatures & Watermarks: Draw, place signatures & brand PDF/image exports.
✓
Page Reorder & 30-Day Trash: Full page control with recovery safety net.
✓
Encrypted Backups: Passphrase AES-256 local archives.
✓
Multilingual & Arabic RTL: Native right-to-left layout and cursive shaping.

Our Engineering Values

Software That Works for You.

The modern app landscape is saturated with surveillance and subscription fatigue. RapidOps was created as an antidote.

1

Silicon Over Servers

Modern smartphones have incredible neural processing chips. We do heavy compute (like OCR and indexing) directly on your device rather than sending personal data over networks.

2

Zero Surveillance

We don't inject analytics trackers, crash scrapers, or third-party ad networks. We have zero interest in monetizing who you are or what documents you scan.

3

Buy Once, Own Forever

Simple utilities don't require recurring monthly charges. We charge a fair, one-time lifetime unlock price, while keeping essential everyday features free.
