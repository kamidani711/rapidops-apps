Privacy Policy for Docket

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Docket does not collect your data. Every document you scan is processed and stored on your device only. Nothing is uploaded to us or to anyone else, and there is no server holding your files.

Who we are

Docket is developed and published by rapidops For Online Selling ("RapidOps"), a business registered in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. You can reach us at hello@rapidops.io.

What Docket does with your documents

When you scan a document, receipt, or warranty card, the image is captured, processed, and saved entirely on your device. Text recognition runs on your device. Receipt parsing runs on your device. Search runs on your device.

We never see your documents. We have no ability to see them. Docket does not transmit document images, extracted text, file names, or any content derived from your documents to us or to any third party.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. Specifically, Docket does not collect or transmit:

  • Your documents, images, or any text extracted from them
  • Your name, email address, phone number, or contact details
  • Your location
  • Your device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • Your usage behaviour or analytics
  • Your contacts, calendar, or other apps' data

Docket does not require an account. There is nothing to sign up for and no profile to create.

Usage statistics stay on your device

Docket keeps a small local record of how often you use certain features, such as how many documents you have scanned. This is stored on your device, is visible to you in the app's settings, can be cleared by you at any time, and is never transmitted anywhere.

Purchases

Docket offers an optional one-time purchase to unlock premium features. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. When you make or restore a purchase, Google receives the information needed to process and verify that transaction, in line with Google's own privacy policy.

We receive confirmation that a purchase was made. We do not receive your payment card details, and we do not link purchases to any of your documents or activity in the app.

Permissions Docket requests

  • Camera — to scan documents. Images go straight into the app's private storage on your device.
  • Storage / Photos — only when you choose to import an existing image or PDF, or export a file to a location you pick.
  • Notifications — only if you enable warranty expiry reminders. These are generated on your device.

Docket does not request location, contacts, microphone, or any other sensitive permission.

Where your data is stored

All Docket data lives in your device's private app storage, which other apps cannot read. If you use the optional backup feature, Docket creates an encrypted archive and you choose where to save it — your own cloud storage, an SD card, or anywhere else you select. That file never passes through us, and we cannot read it.

Deleting your data

You can delete any document from within the app. Uninstalling Docket removes all of its data from your device permanently. Because we hold nothing, there is no account to close and no deletion request to send us.

Children

Docket is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because we do not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If Docket ever changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and revise the date above. If a future version of Docket introduced any feature that transmitted data, we would state that plainly here and in the app before it took effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about Docket: hello@rapidops.io
