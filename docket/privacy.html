Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026 · App: Docket (io.rapidops.docket)

Our Fundamental Privacy Commitment

Docket is built on an offline-first, on-device architecture. We do not operate remote document servers, we do not require account registration, and we do not sync or upload your document images or text to the cloud. Your scans stay on your phone.

1. Document Images and Extracted Text

All document scanning, image enhancement, perspective correction, optical character recognition (OCR), and full-text library indexing occur strictly within the local hardware of your device.

Docket has no backend server or cloud sync service. There is no remote database or infrastructure where your files could be received or stored.

2. Tracking, Advertising & Analytics

Docket contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero advertising identifiers, and zero behavioral tracking networks. We do not track what you scan, when you scan, or who you share documents with.

The app maintains a minimal local-only usage log (such as total documents scanned) solely to display personal storage and usage metrics in your on-device Settings screen. This data is stored in your private local app database and can be cleared by you at any time.

3. Transparency Regarding Google Play & ML Kit Libraries

Docket’s own codebase declares no internet access and opens zero network sockets. However, the app includes official Google Play libraries for:

  • Google Play Billing: To process and verify your one-time lifetime Premium purchase.
  • Google ML Kit Document Scanner: To deliver fast on-device page edge detection and optical character recognition.

These official Google libraries contribute the internet permission in the merged application manifest for license verification and diagnostics. Docket will never transmit your document images, recognized text, or library data across any network.

4. Device Permissions Requested

Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Strictly used to capture pages when you activate the scanner. Camera frames are processed in memory and never streamed elsewhere.
Storage (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 9 and older): Only requested on legacy Android versions when you explicitly choose to export a PDF or image to your device's Downloads directory.

5. Backups & Data Ownership

Docket provides an optional Encrypted Local Backup tool. When you create a backup, you choose the destination (such as an external SD card, USB storage, or your private cloud drive) and provide a passphrase.

The backup file is encrypted on-device. Docket does not hold or store your passphrase; we cannot read, recover, or access your backup files.

6. Contact & Source Inquiries

If you have questions about this privacy statement or Docket's architecture, you can inspect the open codebase or submit questions directly on our GitHub repository: github.com/kamidani711/docket .
